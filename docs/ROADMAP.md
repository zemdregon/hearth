## Product decisions
Working name: Hearth. One family per deployment; no public signup. Internal passkeys are the sole authentication method, with private enrollment and permanent member ids; no passwords, email addresses, OTP or social-login fallback. Family timezone configurable. Mood entries private unless shared. Daily answers record an immutable pair and reveal only when both partners in that currently active pair have answered; separate profile consent releases a preference to the whole family. Larger households require an explicit pair; legacy answers remain author-only. Modules can be disabled without deleting data. AI produces drafts for human review. Demo data is fictional and memory-only. Offline storage contains only the public shell, never private family records.
